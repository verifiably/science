# Evaluate literature

- Model memory is orientation only, never evidence of what a paper says.
- Cross-check identity before writing about a paper. Keep primary versus secondary
  provenance separate from publication status (peer-reviewed, preprint, other).
- Read full text before repeating numbers, cohort definitions, method parameters
  or validation claims. Mark abstract-only reading explicitly; it supports no
  durable conclusion. Do not infer details absent from the text read.
- Check sources for independence before counting agreement: shared data, shared
  cohorts, shared methods and reviews repeating the same result are not independent
  confirmations.
- Synthesize across sources. Name disagreements, what they turn on and gaps;
  do not summarize each source in turn. Separate source statements, world results
  and the reader's own reasoning. Hedge to match evidence without stored strength
  or confidence grades.
