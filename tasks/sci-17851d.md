science opens every attended session with coordination=None (science.serve.serve and science.mcp.serve), so a coordination-class command passes the declaration-time permit check — RequiredCapabilities.coordination() constructs and a full permit covers it — and then refuses CoordinationUnavailable at its act.

Spec §5.1 already anticipates the fix: the constructor takes a compiled ProfileSpec for coordination and 'the launcher's configuration therefore names the contract documents the profile compiles from'. The write profile got that treatment in the 2026-09-09 amendment as the 'domains' config key compiled into ScienceConfig.profile; coordination needs the equivalent. beliefs.corpus.require_profile_compatible refuses a coordination profile differing from the write profile in base identity, activated contracts, or compiled identity, so the two are not independent.

Nothing can act on it yet: the shipped base contract declares no coordination kinds, and the coord-note synthetic exemplar carries an 'unreachable' handler for that reason. Promote when sub-project 1 supplies a coordination contract. sci-498acb is the same shape, waiting on sub-project 5.
