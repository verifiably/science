Keith, 2026-10-18. A second program over reused public cohorts is what forces the general parts of the multiple-myeloma machinery (roster, overlap, exposure, reservation) upstream and shows which parts are myeloma-shaped. The planned second project is health over mm30 (sci-0d00d2); breast cancer is offered as another: never examined in proto-science, so no prior-exposure ledger to inherit, and very large public data (METABRIC, TCGA-BRCA, SCAN-B, thousands of GEO series, with subtype, survival and treatment phenotypes). Costs to weigh: the literature and model priors for breast cancer are as heavy as for myeloma, so the blind-analysis idea filed in mm would matter from day one; and a second biology program should start only after the upstream pieces exist, or it will rebuild them. Scope when picked up: the order between this and the health milestone, and which mm components it would consume rather than copy.
