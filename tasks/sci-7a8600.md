service_server's server_close unlinks the socket, then waits for in-flight requests to finish before the session closes (sci-6ffb7f). In that gap check_socket_path sees no socket, so a second launcher can open its own attended session on the same world while the first is still finishing a write — briefly two live sessions, against projects design §5.1a's one live session per world. The order is deliberate: unlinking first means a SIGKILL during a hung drain leaves no stale socket. Scope whether the overlap matters (the kernel serializes writes per root, and selection lives per session), and if it does, whether to unlink after the drain or hold some other marker. Raised as declined-to-judge by the sci-6ffb7f review.
