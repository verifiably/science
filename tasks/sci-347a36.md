Deferred from the commons 1a final review (Final M4, ledger attached to sci-13050a). When a fetched pin's copy has vanished, adopt downloads again and uses the new artifact and marker without comparing them with pin.artifact/pin.marker, against spec §4.5 ('verifies against the pinned head artifact identity'); sharing.apply_line then silently keeps the first line's fields, so the mounted line can ledger an artifact the kernel did not admit. Refuse a re-fetch whose artifact or marker differs from the pin, and make apply_line raise on a line whose evidence disagrees with the pin.

Established at science 99111f7: adopt.handle reuses a fetched pin's copy only when both root and sibling exist; otherwise _fetch discards stale files and builds evidence from the new download without comparing the old pin. apply_line keeps pin.marker/artifact/world/view and returns early on an unchanged state/source, even if the new line disagrees. The mismatch must therefore be rejected before restore_root can ledger replacement evidence, with a second invariant check in apply_line. See [ref:commons] §4.5/10 and [ref:review-ledger] Final M4.

Done: on a re-fetch for an existing pin, compare the downloaded head artifact identity and marker with the pin before any restore/admit act or sharing line; refuse disagreement explicitly and discard the untrusted replacement copy while preserving the pin and existing ledger. An identical re-fetch must still recover a vanished copy. apply_line must reject conflicting immutable pin evidence before any write or idempotent early return, including same-state and older-state lines; retain monotone transitions, exact replays, existing fetch history/keep, and the supported None-to-source enrichment without silently replacing a known source. Cover marker and artifact mismatches, other immutable evidence disagreements, exact replay, and successful identical recovery. Do not add mirror selection or new trust semantics.

Where: python/src/science/commands/adopt.py (_fetch and its caller), science/publication.py's download result, and science/sharing.py (apply_line and replay); python/tests/test_cmd_adopt.py and test_sharing_registry.py, with test_dispatch_sharing.py for refusal closure if needed. Run just test-one tests/test_cmd_adopt.py tests/test_sharing_registry.py tests/test_dispatch_sharing.py, then just test-fast. Coordinate the refusal envelope with sci-6d4286 if touching its shared boundary; neither task requires the other's implementation to begin.
