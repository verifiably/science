Deferred from the commons 1a final review (Final M4, ledger attached to sci-13050a). When a fetched pin's copy has vanished, adopt downloads again and uses the new artifact and marker without comparing them with pin.artifact/pin.marker, against spec §4.5 ('verifies against the pinned head artifact identity'); sharing.apply_line then silently keeps the first line's fields, so the mounted line can ledger an artifact the kernel did not admit. Refuse a re-fetch whose artifact or marker differs from the pin, and make apply_line raise on a line whose evidence disagrees with the pin.
