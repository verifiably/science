Design a bounded comparative protocol for sci-221697, carrying the user-endorsed discussion into a reviewable method before new pilot reading. Read sci-d3044e and sci-b2755f for the original objective and scientific coverage limits; mm-87d8db carries the field-first correction. Consult the existing sweep skill and current project policies.

Separate two outputs: an externally grounded map of research fronts/questions, and an audit of assumptions in local claims/specs. Local examples must not set the discovery universe. Include descriptive, explanatory/model-comparison, causal, predictive and measurement/identifiability questions where relevant; do not demand every type in every field.

Define a small common report core: question and scope, source passages/identities and author-stated versus analyst-inferred status, competing answers, what their resolution would change, assumptions with applicability and provenance, evidence that could discriminate, and coverage limitations. Keep scientific uncertainty, measurement/model assumptions and retrieval/coverage failures distinct.

Rank scientific importance, uncertainty, consequences of resolution and practical tractability separately before any data-feasibility filter. Local dependency reach, article count and citation prominence are not field importance. Allow ties, partial ordering and insufficient-evidence outcomes; no mandatory three-item shortlist.

Evaluation: a lightweight evidence-landscape map; major research fronts covered/missed against independent agendas or a simple review-based baseline; source independence; sensitivity to starting papers/searches; factual attribution and loss of qualifiers; independent read-only review. A source used to generate questions cannot double as independent evaluation without disclosure. Freeze budgets and stopping rules appropriate to each field, recording access shortfalls; equal article counts are not equal coverage.

Develop with broader MM, sun and biological cycles, then freeze the workflow revision before applying it to a fresh fourth topic (recommend climate; astronomy alternative). The transfer test is workflow generalization, not a claim of independent biological confirmation. Do not tune on the fourth topic then score the revised method on that same material as unseen. Define what would justify new representation/ranking machinery and what a failed or inconclusive pilot means.

Reuse sun phase-2a orientation and the ns cycles heritage without broadening existing approved reading plans silently. Current domain exposure/reservation policies govern all reads, including external assessments. No bulk NLP run, scientific data analysis, governed writes or kernel changes in this task.
