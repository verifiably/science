**Important: section 4's exposure procedure stalls the screen.** mm's ledger counts a person or agent reading phenotype-conditioned results "in any form" as exposure. The spec therefore treats abstracts as possible exposure, defers results reads until cohort accessions are checked, and treats an unknown cohort mapping like a reserved one. But accessions almost never appear in abstracts, so at the 80-item title/abstract stage nearly every mm paper has an unknown mapping and a results-bearing abstract. Followed literally, the rule either forbids reading abstracts at all or turns most of the screen into "possible new exposure, stop" entries that mm's workflow then has to dispose of. A paper that does not map may be read through its data-availability statement, with any accession found there rechecked before its results sections. Whether an abstract read of an unmapped paper counts as exposure is mm policy, so the spec should name that as your ruling rather than decide it.
