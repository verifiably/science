Commit e7b3fa1 (sci-62e5aa) taught Dispatcher._invoke_write to treat any recorded invocation act at a KERNEL_REFUSALS exception as a prior act and raise HandlerContractViolation. Boundary operations (execute_assessment_run, replay, publish) that refuse after opening their intent fulfill that intent with a refusal act-report through the ledgered operation port before returning RunRefused or PublishRefused (raised as KernelRefusalValue). Close such boundary refusals through _close_refused while keeping _close_acted when a substantive prior act committed.
