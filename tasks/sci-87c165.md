Why: beliefs' estimand-typing design (docs/designs/2026-09-12-estimand-typing-design.md, decisions 2 and 6, sections 7.3 and 13) assigns the comparison of applicability against claim qualifiers to a science view. co_scoped and commensurable exist in beliefs.estimand and are not read by belief policy v1. This view should make the held evidence and its limits useful before a successor aggregation policy is chosen.

Scope after sci-4e0f7f: show the target question/claim, assessment estimates and uncertainty, applicability versus claim qualifiers, commensuration differences, and the measurement bindings needed by the worked example in sci-35af80. For each binding expose the measured quantity, unit, time point, population and selection procedure, plus whether the variable is measured in that analysis. Show relevant independence/provenance information when available rather than implying that similarly typed rows are independent.

Acceptance: typed field equality is not a certificate of safe pooling or of semantic measurement correspondence. The existing design expressly leaves the latter authored, and selection hidden in method prose can escape both predicates. Show missing/undeclared information and those limits explicitly. Use a concrete comparison that differs in population/selection and one with equal typed fields but an unresolved measurement or selection assumption. Do not silently widen scope, infer compatibility from names, pool estimates, or change belief.

Where to start: tasks doc beliefs-d6060a estimand, especially limitation 1 (measurement correspondence) and limitation 9 (prose-only selection); beliefs/python/src/beliefs/estimand.py; sci-4e0f7f; sci-33eb94 and sci-1fa085. The weighted-belief research beliefs-8060d0 may consume examples from the view but is not a prerequisite. This remains an idea until the workflow design settles the representation and display.
