Framing §7. A small experimental design, not a system design: a few contrasting measurements chosen after the hctsa review; known constructions observed several ways (§2.3: subsampling, variable selection, aggregation, noise, normalization); and an independently sampled public-data pilot where n=100 counts sampled records before exclusion and eligibility is counted downstream on the denominator ladder. Define the survey sampling frame and the time-series-shaped eligibility rung with inter-rater measurement (§6 q4). For each question, state which observations distinguish the competing explanations and which outcomes leave them unresolved. No closed generator set, no fixed detector count, no primitive ontology. Written with the detector contract of §3.4 (measurement and interpretation as separate fields; each null names what it preserves and which alternative it tests).
