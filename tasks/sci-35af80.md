Why: the September 12 models work separated structure, estimator and generator and built the beliefs foundations, but the daily surface still lacks a complete workflow for using them together. Deliver one real causal question from an authored graph through identification, analysis and interpretation, with every result traceable to its inputs. This goal captures the user's accepted direction; it does not approve an implementation design.

Done: a worked example binds the question and estimand to an exact composite revision, measured quantities, units, time points, population and selection procedure; distinguishes measured and unmeasured variables for the analysis; records assumptions; obtains a precisely qualified identification result; executes one supported analysis; and presents estimates, uncertainty, diagnostics and the applicable belief result without strengthening belief through graph authorship or diagnostics alone. A reproducible result must come back into the workflow, not stop at exported script text. Demonstrate the ordinary success path and the refusal/unsupported paths in the reviewed design. Close only after the example is exercised and its evidence is recorded, not when commands or design documents alone exist.

Boundaries: keep structure, estimator and generator separate in the kernel while presenting them coherently in science. Science owns authoring, visualization and tool orchestration; beliefs owns governed records, identity, provenance, admission and any necessary semantic extensions; autonomy owns execution priorities and scheduling. All durable outputs use existing governed kernel routes unless a separately reviewed extension proves necessary. Do not create a new generic model entity, independent storage, graph-confidence scalar or ladder level.

Ordering: use beliefs-d6060a for semantic prerequisites. Composite presentation, scope comparison and causal-tool integration belong to this milestone. The weighted-belief research beliefs-8060d0, its synthesis beliefs-5e66ba and successor policy beliefs-638318 are not milestone prerequisites. Structural sensitivity, general model criticism and question-directed next are follow-ons, not requirements for closing this first example.
